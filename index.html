Three rules on the bench

  1. i.

    Feedback first

    Every press answers within a frame. Motion explains what just happened, not what the designer wanted to show off.

  2. ii.

    Physical, not literal

    Fur, cloth and jelly are borrowed for how they respond, never for decoration. Springs settle, they never loop.

  3. iii.

    Calm by request

    With reduced motion on, every component still works; the wobble simply steps aside.
